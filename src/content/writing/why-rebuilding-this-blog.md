I’m rebuilding this blog because I want it to be a useful record of work and learning, not a feed that has to stay busy. I’m revisiting a small number of older pieces and keeping them in draft while I check their claims, context, and wording.

The standard is simple: explain what I tried, distinguish observation from opinion, show the trade-offs, and leave room for uncertainty. Some subjects need better sources or another knowledgeable reader before they are ready. Others are better left in the archive.

The first set of essays revisits an early experiment with AI-assisted development and a small visual-story project. I’ll add new writing when I have a concrete question or experience worth explaining.
