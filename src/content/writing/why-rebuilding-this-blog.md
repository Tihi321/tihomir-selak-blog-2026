I’m rebuilding this blog because I want it to be a useful record of work and learning, not a feed that has to stay busy. I revisited all six posts from the old archive, kept the original publication dates visible, and substantially rewrote them where the claims, context, or framing needed work.

The standard is simple: explain what I tried, distinguish observation from opinion, show the trade-offs, and leave room for uncertainty. Some subjects need better sources or another knowledgeable reader before they are ready. Others are better left in the archive.

The archive now includes essays about AI-assisted development, a visual-story experiment, narrative promotion, game studios, decision-making, and what physics means by observation. I’ll add new writing when I have a concrete question or experience worth explaining.
